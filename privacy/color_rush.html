Colour Rush – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Colour Rush ("the app") is published by Quick Brain Games. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email, contacts, location or any account details. Your best score is saved only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, app interactions and approximate location. Google's use of this information is described in its policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change that choice at any time from "Privacy settings" on the start screen. Anyone can reset or delete their advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in their Android device settings under Google > Ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
